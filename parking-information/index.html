Parking and Getting to 13 Cronulla Street

We are in the Cronulla Street precinct in the centre of Cronulla. Here is how to reach us and where to leave the car.

By train

Cronulla station is the terminus of the T4 Eastern Suburbs and Illawarra line, and the practice is a short walk from the platform. For patients coming from Woolooware, Caringbah, Miranda, Sutherland and further up the line, the train is often simpler than driving and parking.

By ferry

The Cronulla to Bundeena ferry runs from Cronulla wharf, a short walk from Cronulla Street. Bundeena and Maianbar patients can plan an appointment around the timetable.

By car

From Caringbah South, Burraneer and Greenhills Beach the drive is a matter of minutes. Woolooware, Taren Point and Kurnell are close by, and the practice is straightforward to reach from the wider Sutherland Shire.

Accessibility

If you have specific access requirements, please mention them when you book and we will make sure everything is ready for you.
