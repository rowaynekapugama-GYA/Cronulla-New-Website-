Under one roof

Most treatment stays under one roof

One of the quieter frustrations of dental care is being referred elsewhere. You attend a check-up, something is found, and you are handed a phone number for a practice on the other side of the Shire. Two more appointments, a new set of forms, and a dentist who has never met you.

The Cronulla Dentists is set up to keep the majority of treatment in one place. From routine check-ups through to crowns, implants and root canal therapy, most of what patients need is available at 13 Cronulla Street from the same team you see for a clean.

Where a referral genuinely serves you better, we will say so and arrange it properly. It will not be the default answer.

We see patients from Cronulla, Woolooware, Burraneer, Greenhills Beach, Caringbah South, Taren Point, Kurnell, Bundeena and across the Sutherland Shire. If you are not sure which appointment you need, call and describe the problem. Reception can book you the right length of visit rather than leaving you to guess from a list of service names.

To arrange an appointment, call us on (02) 8599 9815.
